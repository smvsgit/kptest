SCOPE DELIVERED
Display-only fix on Dashboards & Reports. No schema, route, permission or
business-logic change.

DEFECT
The KPI tile converted a metric to a human unit only when its key contained the
word "storage". integration_capacity_bytes and integration_free_bytes therefore
rendered as raw byte counts (21,89,20,43,89,888 and 9,98,52,01,27,488), which
are unreadable at a glance. storage_bytes was converted, but always to GB, so a
small total showed as 0.02 GB.

FIX
- Every metric key ending in _bytes is now scaled to the largest sensible unit
  (B / KB / MB / GB / TB / PB).
- The exact byte count is kept in the value's tooltip.
- Tiles relabelled: Storage Used, Integration Capacity, Integration Free Space.
  The "Bytes" suffix was misleading once the value carries its own unit.
- Byte formatting consolidated into resources/js/format.ts. Six copies of a
  byte formatter existed elsewhere in the front end and this panel had none,
  which is why these tiles were raw.
